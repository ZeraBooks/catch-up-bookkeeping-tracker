# Catch-up bookkeeping checklist

Work top to bottom. The order matters more than the speed: a month done out of
order has to be redone when the month before it changes.

Business: ____________  Last month you trust: ____________  Catch up through: ____________

## 1. Find the starting point
- [ ] Pick the most recent month whose closing balance in the books matches the bank statement, for every account
- [ ] If no month ties for an account, that account needs a cleanup first, not a catch-up
- [ ] Write the starting balances into the tracker, one row per account

## 2. Gather
- [ ] Bank statements (PDF) for every account, every month since the starting point
- [ ] Credit card statements (PDF), every card, every month
- [ ] Payroll reports, to split each payroll withdrawal into wages, taxes and fees
- [ ] Loan statements, to split each payment into principal and interest
- [ ] Payout reports from sales platforms, to turn net deposits back into sales, fees and refunds
- [ ] The last trusted balance sheet or trial balance

Download PDF statements, not the activity CSV. The CSV usually has no opening
or closing balance, and those balances are what prove a month is complete.

## 3. Work oldest month first, one account at a time
- [ ] Record and categorize the month
- [ ] Reconcile it to the statement's closing balance
- [ ] Mark Imported and Reconciled in the tracker and fill in the books closing
- [ ] Run the status script; fix anything that doesn't tie before the next month

## 4. Missing statements
- [ ] Every gap in the chain listed (a statement that doesn't open where the last one closed)
- [ ] Each missing month requested from the bank now; older ones can take a week or two

## 5. Questions, in one batch
- [ ] Transfers between your own accounts
- [ ] Owner draws and money the owner put in
- [ ] Personal charges on the business card
- [ ] Anything else unclear, with date and amount

## 6. Done
- [ ] Every account reconciled through the last full month
- [ ] From here on, book each month when its statement arrives
